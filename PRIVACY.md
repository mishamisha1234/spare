# Spare — privacy

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

Spare has no accounts, no sign-up, and no analytics SDK. This document says
what actually leaves your phone, where it goes, and what is kept.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you can see in the app. Your library, your recall questions and
their schedule, your points and achievements, your interests, your reading
preferences, and your reminder time all live in Spare's own on-device
database. None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, or readable by anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere to restore from,
which is the other side of having no account.

## What is sent, and why

Spare generates lessons through a proxy we run, which holds the API key so
your device does not have to. Each generation request carries:

- **The topic, length and interest category** you chose. This is what the
  lesson is written from, and it is also the key the shared lesson cache uses,
  so a topic somebody has already read can be served instead of generated
  again. Cache entries are keyed on the topic — not on you — and carry nothing
  about who read them.
- **A random device identifier**, generated on first launch and stored in
  Spare's own app-group storage. It is not your Apple ID, not
  `identifierForVendor`, and not linked to anything else. It exists so the
  daily free allowance and the free trial can be counted per device. Deleting
  and reinstalling the app produces a new one.
- **Your App Store transaction**, if you subscribe. The proxy passes it to
  Apple to confirm the subscription is live, and keeps no copy.

Lessons are generated by Anthropic's API, under Anthropic's terms. We do not
send them anything that identifies you.

## What the proxy keeps

Against your device identifier:

- how many lessons you have read today and this month,
- which cached lessons you have already been shown, so you are not served the
  same one twice,
- your free trial's state: when it started, when it ends, and how much of it
  is left.

That is the whole list. No lesson text, no reading history you could be
identified from, no location, no contacts, no advertising identifiers, and
nothing shared with or sold to anybody.

## The five numbers

Spare keeps five running totals, globally, with **no device identifier
attached to any of them**:

1. free trials started,
2. trials in which at least three lessons were read,
3. paywalls closed without a purchase,
4. purchases made around the end of a trial,
5. purchases made later.

They are integers in a counter. They cannot be traced to a device, they cannot
be broken down by person, and they say nothing about what anybody read. They
exist to answer one question — whether people who are given a free week
actually use it — which cannot be answered from a single phone, because a
share needs a denominator that spans devices.

This is the only thing Spare counts across users, and it is why "no analytics"
is stated here as "no analytics SDK, and five integers" rather than as
nothing at all. If that distinction matters to you, the counters are in
`FunnelLedger` in the open source for this app, and this is all of them.

## Notifications

If you turn on recall reminders, the scheduling happens entirely on your
device. No notification is sent from a server, and we do not know whether one
fired or whether you opened it.

## Children

Spare is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify
anyone, of any age.
